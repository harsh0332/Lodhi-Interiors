concept: We resolved the traditional tension between social entertaining and aromatic Indian cooking by engineering a dual-zone architectural layout. The forward pavilion features an expansive monolithic quartz island, fluted oak breakfast counter, and integrated induction hobs bathed in natural morning light. Behind a set of flush fluted-glass pocket doors lies a secondary heavy-prep scullery with a dedicated 1400 m3/hr industrial centrifugal exhaust hood. Carcasses are constructed from 100% boiling-water-proof (IS 710) marine plywood, edged with 2mm laser-welded PUR banding to eliminate water ingress. Motion is governed entirely by Austrian Blum Servo-Drive electronic opening mechanisms, allowing touch-free drawer access while handling culinary ingredients.

challenges:
  - Routing industrial 8-inch rigid ducting through pre-cast RCC lintels without structural damage.
  - Mitigating 100% relative humidity and oil deposition during heavy summer cooking.
challengesDecisions:
  - constraint: Diamond core-cutting through a pre-stressed structural lintel was prohibited by structural engineering review.
    resolution: Re-routed the 8-inch rigid stainless ducting horizontally through a custom exterior soffit plenum, avoiding all structural load beams and minimizing acoustic fan back-pressure.
  - constraint: Extreme thermal and moisture exposure causing warpage in conventional particleboard modular carcasses.
    resolution: Mandated 100% calibrated 18mm boiling-water-proof (IS 710) marine ply carcasses with waterproof PUR hot-melt edge sealing, providing absolute resistance to tropical humidity.
executionNote: Built, wired, and plumbed by Lodhi Interiors turnkey site technicians. Plumbing lines were pressure-tested to 6 bar before cabinetry installation to guarantee zero concealed wall leaks.
