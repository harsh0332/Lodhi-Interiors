brief: The client commissioned our execution team to build an architecturally demanding residence with extensive double-height volumes in Chuna Bhatti. The assignment required translating intricate structural drawings into site reality with 2mm shadowline tolerances and zero-joint marble floors.
concept: Built upon principles of spatial reduction and material honesty, this residence directs attention toward changing daylight conditions rather than ornamental surface treatments. A continuous central lightwell pulls southern illumination into the heart of the ground-floor living pavilion, illuminating honed Indian white marble floor planes and lime-washed masonry. Custom white oak joinery was fabricated with flush shadowline jambs to ensure partitions read as architectural planes rather than applied decorative panels.
materials:
  - Honed Indian Banswara Marble
  - Natural White Oak
  - Brushed Nickel Ironmongery
  - Breathable Lime Wash Plaster
challenges:
  - Executing precision 2mm shadowline reveals along sloping ceiling angles with custom flush-mount door jambs.
  - Ensuring flawless flush alignment between natural stone floor slabs and wall skirtings without visible silicone beads.
outcome: Refined residential sanctuary delivered with impeccable joinery tolerances, zero-joint stone flooring, and uncompromising site supervision.
