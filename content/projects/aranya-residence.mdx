concept: The original floor plan divided the residence into sealed rooms that blocked cross-ventilation and daylight from reaching the central living spine. Our architectural intervention stripped non-structural masonry walls to establish a continuous visual axis from the entrance foyer directly out to an interior courtyard garden. We chased a feeling of monastic calm grounded in tactile permanence: monolithic Italian travertine floor planes flow uninterrupted across living and dining volumes, establishing a cool datum against Bhopal's intense summer climate. Above, bespoke fluted teak joinery warms vertical elevations, concealing ducted air distribution within dropped architectural reveals rather than decorative false ceilings. The play of light throughout the day transforms the lime-washed plaster walls, catching micro-textures and softening exterior glare without requiring heavy drapery.

challenges:
  - Concealing ducted HVAC infrastructure without lowering the original 11-foot structural ceiling slab.
  - Ensuring complete acoustic and dust isolation from Arera Colony's main road corridor.
  - Mitigating an 8-week transport delay on imported travertine during monsoon highway disruptions.
challengesDecisions:
  - constraint: Concealing ducted VRV HVAC infrastructure without compromising the original 11-foot ceiling clearances.
    resolution: Engineered custom perimeter shadowline service chases along structural beam lines, maintaining full 11-foot clear height across living and entertaining zones.
  - constraint: Heavy dust infiltration and acoustic disturbance from nearby arterial traffic in Arera Colony.
    resolution: Installed thermally broken double-glazed acoustic fenestration with magnetic drop-down drop seals, achieving an acoustic reduction rating of STC 48.
  - constraint: Imported Navona travertine slabs faced an eight-week transport delay due to regional monsoon flooding.
    resolution: Re-sequenced on-site trade coordination to finalize all civil modifications, electrical cabling, and millwork carcasses in advance, dry-laying stone within 9 days of delivery.
executionNote: Managed end-to-end under single-source turnkey custody by Lodhi Interiors. Our in-house carpentry and masonry crews maintained continuous daily presence on site in Arera Colony, executing 1.5mm shadowline tolerances and zero-joint stone alignment.
