concept: Our design approach focused on framing the horizontal expanse of Shahpura Lake while shielding interior volumes from western solar heat gain. We eliminated conventional wall partitions on the main level, replacing them with freestanding smoked oak millwork volumes that organize circulation without closing down sightlines. Seamless neutral microcement floors flow continuously from the entrance foyer through to the lakeside terrace, visually extending internal space toward the water horizon. Floor-to-ceiling motorized linen sheers filter high-noon glare into soft ambient glow, while deeply recessed brass light channels provide glare-free illumination as night settles over Bhopal's lake district.

challenges:
  - Mitigating intense afternoon western solar heat gain across 18 meters of lakefront structural glazing.
  - Stabilizing 400 square meters of seamless continuous microcement against seasonal thermal contraction.
challengesDecisions:
  - constraint: Direct western solar radiation across 18m of lakefront glass caused interior temperatures to spike significantly in summer.
    resolution: Engineered deep exterior architectural overhangs combined with motorized low-emissivity pocket screens concealed in ceiling plenums.
  - constraint: High seasonal humidity fluctuation around Shahpura Lake threatened hairline cracking across large unbroken microcement expanses.
    resolution: Installed dual-layer fiber-reinforced screeds with concealed perimeter expansion shadowlines beneath baseboards, ensuring zero crack propagation over three seasonal cycles.
executionNote: Executed over 14 months under single-source turnkey contract by Lodhi Interiors, coordinating civil waterproofing, custom acoustic glazes, and bespoke in-house millwork.
