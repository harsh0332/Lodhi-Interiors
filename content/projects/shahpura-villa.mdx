challenges:
  - Integrating heavy fluted quartzite slabs onto vertical bathroom surfaces with seamless concealed service plumbing.
  - Aligning precision moulding profiles across uneven plaster base substrates without visible caulking lines.
challengesDecisions:
  - constraint: Installing heavy textured quartzite slabs on the vanity wall required structural load support and hidden plumbing access.
    resolution: Built an internal reinforced structural steel sub-frame with magnetic inspection panels concealed within the shadowline perimeter.
  - constraint: Plaster tolerances across existing masonry walls created minor unevenness for straight moulding lines.
    resolution: Our in-house joiners leveled the entire wall datum with precision backing strips, ensuring zero gap across all 140 meters of moulding runs.
