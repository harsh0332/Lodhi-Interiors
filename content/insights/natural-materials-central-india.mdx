# Specifying Natural Stones for Central India's Climate

Bhopal experiences sharp diurnal and seasonal temperature variations. Material choices that perform effortlessly in temperate zones require careful acclimatization, substrate preparation, and vapor-permeable sealers when installed in Central India.
